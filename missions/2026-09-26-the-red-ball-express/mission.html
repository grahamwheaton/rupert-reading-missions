The Red Ball Express

FACT

It was August 1944, during World War Two.

Allied armies were moving quickly across France.

But there was a problem.

Tanks need fuel. Soldiers need food. Guns need ammunition.

And the supplies were far behind them.

Many railway lines had been damaged during the fighting.

So the United States Army made a giant road delivery system.

It was called the Red Ball Express.

The name came from an old railway phrase.

A red ball meant that important freight should move quickly.

The first Red Ball trucks rolled on 25 August 1944.

They carried fuel, food, ammunition and other supplies from Normandy towards the advancing armies.

The trucks followed special roads.

Loaded trucks travelled one way.

Empty trucks returned on another route.

This made a huge loop across France.

Red circles marked the route.

Other traffic was kept away so the supply trucks could keep moving.

Driving was hard work.

Some roads were narrow and damaged.

Trucks ran during the day and at night.

Engines overheated. Tyres wore out. Drivers became tired.

Mechanics were vital.

If a truck broke down, they tried to repair it quickly.

A stopped truck meant its cargo was not moving forwards.

At the busiest point, nearly 6,000 vehicles were working on the Red Ball routes.

On 29 August, they moved more than 12,000 tons of supplies in a single day.

A fact worth remembering

About three quarters of the Red Ball soldiers were African American.

The United States Army was still segregated then, which meant Black and white soldiers were often kept in separate units.

Black soldiers were frequently given transport and supply jobs.

Their work on the Red Ball Express became a crucial part of keeping the armies supplied.

The drivers did not usually fight with tanks at the front.

Yet their job mattered enormously.

A powerful tank with an empty fuel tank cannot move at all.

The Red Ball Express ran until 16 November 1944.

In less than three months, its trucks carried more than 400,000 tons of supplies.

This sort of work is called logistics.

Logistics means getting the right people and supplies to the right place at the right time.

It may not sound as dramatic as a tank battle.

But without logistics, an army soon stops.
